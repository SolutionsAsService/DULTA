Microcontroller

embedded / registers · gpio · timing · debounce · pwm

A simulated 8-bit microcontroller. The LED is on PB5; a button on PB0 connects to ground. Write firmware against the registers io.DDRB (1 = output), io.PORTB (output level, or pull-up for an input) and io.PINB (read pins), plus millis(), micros() and delay(ms). Each pass of loop() takes 10 µs, and the chip runs for 5 simulated seconds.

Registers appear after a run.
